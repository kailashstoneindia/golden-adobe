#QuestionWhat the system does todayIf the answer differs
1
After a failed delivery, where do the goods go that night: back to the vendor, a warehouse, or stay on the truck?
TodayA re-attempt assumes the goods stay on the truck. There is no return or warehouse state.
If differentA return job, a warehouse stop or a "received" scan, plus new statuses.
2
How many delivery attempts are allowed, and who pays for a repeat?
TodayNo limit. Ops can re-attempt as often as needed.
If differentAn attempt cap, and a fee or refund rule.
3
Do they want the customer's delivery code (OTP)?
TodayBuilt in. The customer app shows it and the driver must enter it. SMS is off.
If differentIf kept, SMS needs a DLT template. If dropped, the driver closes with the photo only.
4
Who unloads at the site: the driver, the helper or the customer?
TodayThe loading and unloading fee is still ₹0 (decision 0036).
If differentAn unloading step and a fee in the flow.
5
Is there a delivery fee for customers?
TodayCheckout charges none.
If differentA fee line at checkout and on the order.
6
Does the helper need an identity check (Aadhaar or licence)?
TodayNone in the MVP. Only drivers have documents.
If differentA document step like the driver's.
7
What should the customer be told when a delivery fails, and by what channel?
TodayOps phone the customer. The app only shows "Delivery attempt failed".
If differentA notification template and a send step.
